TITLE
Cursed Encounters - Pirate Affixes

SHORT SUMMARY
Spice up on-foot combat with random enemy traits, tougher Elites and configurable rewards. Includes an F8 settings panel and optional HP numbers. Works alongside money-drop multiplier mods.

DESCRIPTION - NEXUS BBCODE
[b]Want to spice up your combat a bit? Need more of a challenge?[/b]

Cursed Encounters gives eligible street enemies, minibosses, bounties and bosses a random chance to spawn with combat-changing affixes. Two or more traits earn the [color=#B87DE8][b]Elite[/b][/color] title. Expect tougher fights, dangerous combinations and rewards you can tune to your liking.

[b]Meet the traits[/b]
[color=#F5A623][b]Strong[/b][/color] - More health and harder hits.
[color=#71B7FF][b]Speedy[/b][/color] - Faster animations to keep you on your toes.
[color=#6DC86D][b]Regen[/b][/color] - Restores health over time. Keep the pressure on.
[color=#F5A623][b]Berserk[/b][/color] - Hits harder below its configured health threshold.
[color=#F5A623][b]Enraged[/b][/color] - Gets faster as health falls, but takes more damage.
[color=#71B7FF][b]Heavyweight[/b][/color] - A slower, tougher bruiser with armor against hit reactions.
[color=#F5A623][b]Volatile[/b][/color] - Leaves a delayed fiery blast after defeat. Give it some space!

[b]Your combat, your rules[/b]
Press [b]F8[/b] for a settings panel with individual trait pages. Adjust chances, strengths, regeneration, explosions and rewards. Press [b]F7[/b] to toggle current/max HP numbers above enemy health bars. Settings save to the INI automatically.

Each trait is worth [b]$500 base by default[/b], with individual reward values and a configurable drop percentage, defaulting to 100%. Multiple trait bonuses combine into [b]one cash pickup[/b]. Original game drops remain separate and unchanged.

[b]Works in tandem with a money-drop multiplier mod if desired.[/b] For example, two default traits give $1,000 base, or $10,000 with the tested x10 money table. No money multiplier is required.

[b]Installation[/b]
Close the game. Extract the ZIP and copy PirateAffixes.asi and PirateAffixes.ini beside likeadragonpirates.exe in runtime\media. Requires a working ASI loader, such as the one used with ShinRyuModManager/Parless. Keep earlier affix prototypes disabled. Full settings and removal instructions are included in README-FIRST.txt.

[b]Compatibility[/b]
Built for the supported Windows Steam 1.14 executable and tested with English game text. Uses runtime changes rather than replacing the reward database. The F8 panel is a separate native window over the game; borderless/windowed mode is recommended. Scripted encounters may have their own restrictions. Chance and HP-scale edits affect newly spawned enemies.
